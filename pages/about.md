Hi, I am Dominic. I live in the UK.

I have loved technology for as long as I can remember. I like to take things apart, find out how they work, and build something new with them. Today most of that is AI. I think it is the most exciting technology of my life, and I like to find practical uses for it.

Away from the screen, I keep bees. The apiary is a good place to unwind and think. I also brew beer, and I enjoy drinking it. Both hobbies have a habit of turning into tech projects.

Gardens of AI is where I write up the things I build. Each post is one idea, from the first reason to build it to what worked and what did not.

You can find me on [LinkedIn](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/dominic-searle-1661584).
