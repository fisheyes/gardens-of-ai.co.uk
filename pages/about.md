Hi, I'm Dominic.

I've always been the sort of person who takes things apart to see how they work, and these days most of what I tinker with is AI. Not the hype, but the practical side: what it can actually do when you point it at a real problem, and where it falls over.

This blog is where I write those experiments up, the ones that work and the ones that don't. Some are full projects. Others are short technical notes with code you can run yourself.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm usually with my bees, brewing beer, or playing the guitar badly.
